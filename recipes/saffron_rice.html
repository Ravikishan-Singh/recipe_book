Saffron Rice

Description

Indian Saffron Rice (Kesar Chawal) is a fragrant, celebratory dish where premium Basmati rice is simmered with saffron, ghee, and whole spices.Known for it's delicate floral aroma and natural golden colour, it's a staple at weddings, festivals and other celebratory occasions.

It has a subtle earthy and mildly sweet flavour with a distinct warm and musky floral scent derived from high quality saffron.

Ingredients

  • 3 pinches saffron
  • ¼ cup milk (slightly warmed)
  • 1½ cups white basmati rice, washed
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon black cumin seeds
  • 1½-inch cinnamon stick
  • 4 green cardamom pods
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 6 black cloves
  • 1½ teaspoons salt
  • 2½ cups water

Steps

  1. Wash 1.5 cups of basmati rice thoroughly 3 to 4 times until the water runs clear. Soak the rinsed rice in fresh water for 20 to 30 minutes, then drain the water completely
  2. Crush a generous pinch of saffron strands (about 2 to 3 pinches) into a fine powder using a mortar and pestle. Mix the crushed saffron into 2 to 3 tablespoons of warm milk or warm water and let it steep for at least 15 to 20 minutes to release its color and aroma.
  3. Heat 3 tablespoons of ghee in a deep saucepan or pot over medium heat. Lower the heat and add whole spices: 1 inch cinnamon, 2-3 green cardamoms, 2-3 cloves, and 1 tej patta (Indian bay leaf). Let them sizzle and become fragrant for about 30 seconds.
  4. Add the drained, soaked rice to the pot. Stir gently for 1 to 2 minutes so that every rice grain is glistening and coated in the ghee. Be careful not to break the fragile rice grains.
  5. Cook it for another 15 minutes and add saffron milk on top of it.
  6. Serve it hot and enjoy!!
